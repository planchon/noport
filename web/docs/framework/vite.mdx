---
title: "Vite"
description: "Run your Vite app through noport"
---

<Note>
`noport` will automatically detect the `vite` framework when you run your app through `noport`.
</Note>

When you run your `vite` app through `noport`, we will automatically injects the folowing configuration:

```bash
--host 127.0.0.1 --port RANDOM_PORT
```

Your app will be served on `http://127.0.0.1:{RANDOM_PORT}` internally. You will access your app through the described URL.
